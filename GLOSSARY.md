# Cadence

An online course platform where instructors publish courses and Students buy and work through them.

## People

**Student**:
Someone who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Learner, customer, buyer

## Purchasing

**Purchase**:
One payment that buys access to one course, at the price paid on that day.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Seat**:
One place on a team's Purchase of a course. A Seat is Claimed when a team member takes it, and a Claimed Seat belongs to the team, not to the Student who claimed it.
_Avoid_: Redeemed seat, licence

**Seat Code**:
The token a team member Redeems to Claim a Seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon, invite code, claimed code

## Refunds

**Refund**:
A Student's request to get back the money paid for a Purchase, from the moment it is asked for until it is Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can still be given a Refund right now, judged on time since the Purchase, how much of the course the Student has used, and whether the Purchase is a Claimed Seat.

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a Purchase in which a Refund can be asked for, counting the whole of the last day.

**Refund Amount**:
The money a Refund gives back: the price actually paid for a Purchase made at a purchasing-power price, otherwise the course's list price.

**Requested**:
A Refund that has been asked for and is waiting for someone on the team to decide on it.
_Avoid_: Pending, Open

**Approved**:
A Refund the team has accepted, so the Refund Amount goes back to the card that paid.

**Declined**:
A Refund the team has turned down, so no money goes back.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder raises with their bank to pull back a payment, without going through us. Not the same as a Refund. Use this word only for that.
